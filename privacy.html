Classroom Studio privacy policy

Classroom Studio (a desktop app) and Talk and shoot (its phone app) are used by their owner only, on the owner's own computers, phone and Google account. This page says what they do with Google user data.

What Google data the apps use

  • Google Calendar: the names of the owner's calendars, so the owner can choose one, and the events Classroom Studio creates in that calendar (the owner's timetable).
  • Google Drive: only the files and folders the apps create themselves: a folder in the owner's Drive through which Talk and shoot sends the owner's own recordings (sound, photos and short clips) from the owner's phone to the owner's computer. The apps do not read any other Drive files.

How the data is used

Only to provide these features to the owner. Recordings collected from the Drive folder are processed on the owner's computer. To write the owner's notes, a transcript, and descriptions and pictures from a recording, may be sent to an AI service (the Anthropic API) using the owner's own account; nothing else is shared.

The data is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models by the apps, and not shared with anyone else. There are no analytics or tracking.

Where it is kept

On the owner's own computers and phone, and in the owner's own Google account. Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or with Disconnect in Classroom Studio's settings.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The apps' use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
